Privacy Policy

Kate Pedak Permanent Makeup

Last Updated: February 2026

At Kate Pedak Permanent Makeup (“we,” “us,” or “our”), your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit https://pedak.beauty or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Book an appointment or submit a contact form
  • Communicate with us via phone, text message, email, or social media
  • Complete a consultation or intake form

This information may include:

  • Name and contact information (phone number, email address)
  • Appointment preferences and history
  • Health-related information relevant to your procedure (such as allergies, skin conditions, or medications)
  • Photos of your procedure (before/after), only with your consent

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Schedule, confirm, and manage appointments
  • Send appointment reminders and service-related communications
  • Provide pre-care and after-care instructions
  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Improve our services and overall client experience

With your explicit consent, before-and-after photos may be used for our portfolio, website, or social media.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share information only:

  • With trusted service providers (such as scheduling or payment platforms) who are required to protect your data
  • When required by law or legal process

4. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, including secure storage and limited access to authorized parties only. While no online transmission is 100% secure, we follow industry-standard practices to safeguard your data.

5. Your Rights

You may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request corrections or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications at any time

To make a request, please contact us using the information below.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external platforms such as Instagram. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. Please review their policies separately.

7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve functionality and user experience. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies if you prefer.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
