4. Medical Eligibility & Contraindications

Permanent makeup is a form of cosmetic tattooing and is not suitable for everyone.

You are not eligible if you:

  • Are pregnant or nursing
  • Have a history of keloids or hypertrophic scarring
  • Are currently using Accutane or have used it within the past 12 months
  • Are using Retinol or Retin-A near the treatment area (must discontinue at least 7 days prior)
  • Have an active skin infection, irritation, or open wound near the treatment area
  • Are undergoing chemotherapy or radiation (doctor’s clearance required)
  • Have a bleeding disorder or are taking blood-thinning medications
  • Have diabetes (doctor’s clearance required)
  • Have had Botox in the treatment area within the past 2 weeks
  • Have had dermal fillers in the treatment area within the past 4 weeks

All medical conditions, allergies, and medications must be disclosed during consultation. Failure to disclose relevant information releases us from liability.

We reserve the right to refuse service if a procedure is deemed unsuitable.

5. Informed Consent

Before your procedure, you will be required to sign a consent form confirming that:

  • You understand permanent makeup involves depositing pigment into the skin
  • Results vary based on individual factors and aftercare compliance

  • You have disclosed your medical history accurately and completely

6. Results & Expectations

  • Permanent makeup is semi-permanent and will fade over time.
  • Healed results depend on skin type, lifestyle, sun exposure, and aftercare.
  • Specific results or color retention cannot be guaranteed.
  • Additional sessions may be required to achieve desired outcomes.
  • Following all pre-care and after-care instructions is essential.

7. Touch-Up Policy
